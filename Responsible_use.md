# Responsible Use Guidelines

## Appropriate Use

The churn score should be used to:

- Prioritize retention campaigns
- Identify at-risk customers
- Support marketing decisions
- Assist customer success teams

The model output is advisory only.

---

## Inappropriate Use

Do not:

- Automatically deny services
- Make financial decisions
- Replace human judgment
- Assume predictions are always correct

---

## Human Oversight

Retention teams should review:

- Customer history
- Recent activity
- Business context

before taking action.

---

## Fairness

The model should be periodically audited for:

- Bias
- Data drift
- Performance degradation
